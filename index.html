💡 Why *99# Works Without Internet

USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) operates exclusively over the signalling channel (SDCCH) of the GSM cellular network, completely bypassing packet-switched mobile data (GPRS/3G/4G/5G). It functions even when mobile data is toggled OFF or in areas with 0 bars of internet.

🛡️ Security & Zero Handset Storage

Unlike browser or internet app caching, USSD dialogues are session-based. The moment a user closes the USSD dialog, no financial or PIN data remains stored in the handset memory. Dual-factor authentication is tied to the physical SIM card (IMSI) + 4/6-digit NPCI UPI PIN.
